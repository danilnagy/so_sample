# Single-tone measurement baseline

1. Restore restore.wav with usual stereo settings; keep write off and both knobs untouched
2. Before playing, record full capture.wav at the same reduced level with one second trailing silence
3. Start a separate playback.wav recording, leave about one second silence, then press PLAY; record12 to15 seconds of Pattern1 and stop
4. Confirm whether you hear one tone repeating per loop; do not trim leading silence or the first note, press FX, or move knobs
